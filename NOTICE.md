# Data and research notice

OPSSAT-AD is published by its authors on Zenodo and is associated with the benchmark paper listed in the README. Obtain the dataset directly from the official record and follow its licence/attribution requirements. This repository does not bundle the dataset.

OrbitGuard is an educational/research prototype. Its model outputs are anomaly candidates for human review and must not be treated as validated flight-safety controls, root-cause diagnosis, or live spacecraft telemetry.
